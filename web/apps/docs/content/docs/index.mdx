## Sonivo

Welcome to the Sonivo documentation. Sonivo helps music and choir groups organize
their repertoire, setlists, events and practice in one place.

## Sections

- **Getting started** — set up your group.
- **Groups & members** — roles, invitations and permissions.
- **Repertoire** — songs, arrangements and resources.
- **Scheduling** — setlists, events and RSVP.
