# Practice word-timed lyrics — open-source research

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Scope:** Public Apple Music documentation and public GitHub projects relevant to word/syllable-level lyrics, focus animation, and timed instrumental breaks.
**Classification:** Research and implementation recommendation; this document does not authorize an implementation, dependency, or license exception.

## Findings

### Apple Music Sing

- **FACT — the visible interaction is timed lyric highlighting.** Apple documents real-time lyrics, vocals adjustment, independently animated background vocals, and a duet layout. Its Apple TV support page describes word-by-word timed lyrics; the original Sing announcement describes every syllable as synchronized with the music. Availability depends on the track and supported device. [Apple Music Sing announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/12/apple-introduces-apple-music-sing/) · [Apple TV lyric and Sing support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105015)
- **FACT — Apple does not publish the internal rendering or alignment algorithm.** Public docs describe the product behavior. MusicKit's public `Song` model exposes `hasLyrics`, a Boolean, and the Apple Music API song attributes describe that lyrics are available; these are not a public timed-lyrics feed for a third-party renderer. [MusicKit `Song.hasLyrics`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/musickit/song/haslyrics) · [Apple Music API song attributes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applemusicapi/songs/attributes-data.dictionary)
- **FACT — this research found no Apple feature contract for arbitrary user-added instrumental-region markers.** Sonivo should represent a break as an explicit, owner-authored timed section in its own arrangement data; a blank interval between words alone cannot tell whether the passage is an instrumental, a held vocal, or a pause.

## Recommendation for Sonivo

Implement the renderer natively in the existing React Stage rather than adopting the Apple-like AGPL project. Reuse Sonivo's local audio clock, chord/lyric source, Stage Mode window, brand tokens, and authorization. The UI behavior can be inspired by common karaoke conventions without copying Apple assets, exact screens, brand colors, or source code.

### Data and authoring

- The current `ChordTimingJson` is a list of `{ lineIndex, atMs }` marks. It can identify a current line, but cannot identify a word, align a held syllable, or distinguish an instrumental break from a quiet gap. The current phase explicitly says not to imply word-level timing when these marks are absent.
- A future versioned timing format should hold author-entered word or syllable intervals and explicit instrumental intervals, each with `startMs` and `endMs` against the Arrangement's uploaded audio. Preserve existing line marks and read them as a fallback; do not fabricate word timings from line timing or BPM.
- Keep the initial editor manual and tied to the existing uploaded Arrangement audio: play, tap/keyboard-mark the start and end of each word or instrumental section, preview, nudge timing, and save. Automatic speech recognition, lyrics fetching, audio analysis, and external lyric databases stay out.
- An instrumental passage should be a real interval `{ type: instrumental, startMs, endMs }` in the timing document. During it, the stage can show a restrained “Instrumental”/“Interludio” label, keep the next line faintly visible, and continue the song clock; silence or a skipped line should not be mistaken for a marked instrumental.
- Only use lyrics the Group provides or has a right to use. Do not fetch/copy Apple Music, NetEase, Spotify, or community database lyric text into Group arrangements. Open source code licenses do not grant rights to the lyrics/audio included by a separate database or provider.

### Stage presentation

- Center the active lyric line and word. Let completed words settle into the Group's primary color; animate the current word's fill in time; keep upcoming words muted.
- Scale the active line slightly and use a restrained brand-colored glow. Nearby lines can dim and receive a very light blur so the singer can focus; preserve enough contrast to keep their text readable and avoid making blur the only distinction.
- Maintain a low-noise background from the Group's existing colors. Keep player chrome out of the second-screen display; retain controls in the main window.
- During instrumental intervals, replace the active lyric with a small section label and a calm progress cue. Avoid extra panels, score widgets, pitch indicators, confetti, and synchronized multi-device controls.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; use opacity/position changes without scale/blur motion when reduced motion is requested. Keep contrast, keyboard control in the editor, and a non-animated lyric fallback.

## Sonivo scope and gate

`PHASE-PLAY-SYNC-SPEC.md` and ADR-0031 originally authorized highlighting the current line or block only. The owner explicitly approved a superseding scope on 2026-10-19; ADR-0096 and T-SYNC-06 now authorize manual word timing and explicit instrumental intervals. ADR-0050 remains unchanged: Enhanced LRC word timing stays lossy in v1. Karaoke scoring remains OUT under ADR-0037. The approval does not extend to importing an AGPL/GPL library, lyric data set, or paid service.

## Sonora implementation review — 2026-10-29

**Reviewed snapshot:** `sonorahq/sonora` commit `b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21` (2026-10-28). This review follows the lyrics data model, parsing/ranking, player-side lyrics sheet, fullscreen reuse, and lyric-related changelog/commit history. It does not claim a line-by-line audit of unrelated Sonora subsystems.

### Observed in source

- **One model preserves lyric shape.** `Lyrics` distinguishes plain text from timed lines. A timed line can contain word timings, multiple secondary/background lanes, and a voice role. Each lane has its own start/end and optional word timings. This allows simultaneous lead and backing vocals without flattening them into one string. [`models.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/music/src/models.rs)
- **Backing vocals are children of a lead line.** TTML parsing maps `x-bg` spans to secondary lanes, ignores translation/romanization as spoken words, recognizes speaker agents, and falls back from paragraph times to word/lane times. LRC parsing extracts parenthetical spans into separately timed lanes while preserving punctuation and word placement. [`ttml.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/music/src/lyrics/ttml.rs) · [`lrc.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/music/src/lyrics/lrc.rs)
- **A backing lane waits for both its parent and its own start.** The renderer places lanes directly below their lead line, keeps room for them only while the parent line is arriving, active, or departing, and fades/expands that room with the line transition. A lane's active color and word sweep begin at its own timestamp. This is why a corito can appear small beneath the sung line only when it is about to be heard. [`aside.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/views/src/chrome/aside.rs)
- **Blur follows visual distance, not verse number.** Sonora computes each row's position within the viewport, applies a continuous depth curve, and recomputes it from the drawn position during scroll/animation. Pointer focus clears haze. Far-off settled rows can become measured spacers; row blur is quantized only to quarter-pixel steps to reuse render layers, after whole-pixel steps visibly jumped. [`aside.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/views/src/chrome/aside.rs) · [blur precision fix](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/5b976386)
- **Manual scroll yields control.** Sonora pins follow until the scroll offset changes, resets the inactivity timer with every movement, and resumes only after four seconds without further movement. Trackpad scroll received a separate fix because it did not trigger the mouse-wheel path. [`aside.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/views/src/chrome/aside.rs) · [trackpad follow fix](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/66fc0cf6)
- **Word fill has a soft trailing edge across the line.** The karaoke renderer keeps the highlight's softened landing across word boundaries. It avoids splitting or resetting the edge when one sung word arrives as multiple provider tokens, such as `nothing` → `no` + `thing`. It paces redraws at 45 Hz and adjusts cadence for battery saving. [`aside.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/views/src/chrome/aside.rs) · [frame cap](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/15be38f0)
- **Concurrent lines remain concurrent.** Active-line lookup returns every overlapping voice, not only the latest start. Background karaoke has its own timing and softer tint. A long enough gap between sung lines becomes an explicit instrumental row; no lyrics and an explicitly instrumental track render distinct states. [`lyrics/mod.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/music/src/lyrics/mod.rs) · [`aside.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/views/src/chrome/aside.rs)
- **Provider failures do not become lyric content.** Sonora ranks word-timed above line-timed above plain; uses source priority within a quality class; checks title, artist, and known duration; deduplicates identical sheets; rejects noisy all-caps results; distinguishes “instrumental” from “not found”; retries sources that returned nothing; and fetches the next track's lyrics before it is needed. [`lyrics/mod.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/music/src/lyrics/mod.rs) · [provider guidance](https://sonorahq.org/docs/providers)
- **Malformed timing is bounded and recoverable.** LRC tests cover offsets, hours, alternate hundredths, repeated timestamps, terminal timestamps, invalid/out-of-range marks, blank gaps, headers/credits, instrumentals, and parenthetical lanes. TTML parser requires parseable XML, ignores non-spoken asides, and clamps end times to start. See [`lrc.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/music/src/lyrics/lrc.rs) and [`ttml.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/music/src/lyrics/ttml.rs).
- **Fullscreen is the same lyrics sheet, not another renderer.** The fullscreen shell embeds the shared `Aside`, retains lyrics/queue panel choice, and layers title, artwork, seek, volume and controls around it. This keeps lyric behavior and controls consistent between sidebar and fullscreen. [`fullscreen.rs`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/crates/views/src/shells/fullscreen.rs)

### What the history says, and why

- **2026-08-15–27:** lyrics moved from plain line-follow to blurred stage-like rows, then word sweeps, explicit instrumentals, overlap handling, background vocals, click-to-seek and spring follow. Fixes followed observed failures: a word split caused the color edge to jump backward; measuring each fragment caused CJK load hitches; resizing text every animation frame caused dropped frames and layout shifts; rounding blur to whole pixels produced visible steps. The source records the rationale in [CHANGELOG 0.15–0.20](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/CHANGELOG.md).
- **2026-08-27:** manual-scroll ownership, spring settling, row-drift correction, long-seek blur continuity and lane-height measurement were each fixed separately. These commits show that scroll, visual interpolation, layout reservation and timing are separate failure surfaces: [manual scroll](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/66fc0cf6), [settle through blur](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/9752a8b0), [karaoke frame cap](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/15be38f0).
- **2026-10-11:** virtualization was not applied blindly. Settled offscreen rows are replaced by spacers only after layout has been measured and only while viewport, font, width, size and row state still match. Lyrics remain fully rendered where active animation or interaction requires them. [commit `87134ff4`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/87134ff4) · [commit `070b1028`](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/commit/070b1028).
- **2026-10-26:** readability and failure recovery continued after the visual system matured: unsynced lyrics/romanization improved, and empty provider results became retryable. See [latest changelog entries](https://github.com/sonorahq/sonora/blob/b525b2a702ed81035a2439c29da1ca8158a8ee21/CHANGELOG.md).

### Sonivo comparison and changes made

- **Observed in Sonivo:** `SyncedLyricText` is already shared by player and Stage and advances against the audio clock. Stage and its separate display publish active/word timing, but manual scroll only disabled follow; distance blur remained around a selected line. Fullscreen player likewise stopped following while retaining its audio-derived past-line blur.
- **Changed here:** manual scroll clears distance haze in Stage, its secondary window and fullscreen player. Hover or keyboard focus now clears haze on only that lyric row, eases in a restrained brand-tinted surface, and restores the playback haze after exit; it does not change playback focus or auto-follow. Parenthetical backing lines collapse and expand below their parent, use their own timing when present, and inherit the parent's active state only when untimed. The shared lyric renderer adds a soft orange trail after timed words and a progress-driven glow to the active word. No provider lookup, timing inference, API or storage change was added.
- **Still different from Sonora:** Stage blur still uses lyric-row distance rather than measured viewport distance, and the word sweep remains constrained to each timed word. Sonora's viewport physics, renderer and provider recovery are product-specific; this implementation keeps Sonivo's existing timeline and authorization boundaries.

### Sonivo boundaries

ADR-0100/0101 already permit simultaneous lines, word timing, instrumental intervals, and independently implemented presentation patterns. They keep the validated Arrangement timeline and existing authorization boundaries. This research does not authorize new lyric providers, schema/API changes, automated timing, or third-party lyric payloads. Sonora source paths are reference evidence; implementation remains in Sonivo's existing React/i18n components.
